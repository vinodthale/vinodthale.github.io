I am **Vinod Ashok Thale**, a postdoctoral researcher in **Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)**, with expertise in **interfacial flows**, **drop impact dynamics**, and **thermo-fluid simulations**. My research lies at the intersection of fundamental fluid physics and advanced numerical modeling, aiming to understand multiphase flow phenomena that are often challenging to capture experimentally.

### 🎯 Research Interests

- Multiphase and interfacial fluid dynamics  
- Drop impact with bubble, spreading, and breakup mechanisms , aerodynamic droplet breakup
- Surface wettability and micro-scale phenomena  
- High-fidelity numerical simulations and HPC applications
- Thermal-fluid interactions, heat and mass transfer  
---

### 🧪 Current Research Focus

My ongoing work investigates:
- Aerodynamic droplet breakup  
- Capillary and inertial interactions in fast transients  
- Data-driven modeling of multiphase transport using validated numerical tools  
- Application of CFD in energy, environmental, and manufacturing processes
