I am a computational fluid dynamics (CFD) researcher with expertise in multiphase and interfacial flows, focusing on drop and bubble dynamics, aerodynamic breakup, topological transitions, and more recently, bio-inspired flow simulations.

I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at Zhejiang University, where I work on CFD-based analysis and optimization of recirculating aquaculture systems (RAS). Alongside this, I am developing fish schooling simulations using the IBAMR open-source software framework, employing immersed boundary methods with adaptive mesh refinement (AMR) to study hydrodynamic interactions and collective swimming behavior.

My recent work at IIT Hyderabad involved conducting high-fidelity direct numerical simulations (DNS) of aerodynamic breakup in non-spherical droplets, utilizing the Volume-of-Fluid (VOF) method implemented in the Basilisk framework.

During my PhD at Xi’an Jiaotong University, I investigated the dynamics of air-in-liquid compound droplets, uncovering the mechanisms behind jetting, cavity collapse, and vortex-driven deformation processes.

This site features my research publications, simulation visualizations, technical skills, and curriculum vitae. My main research interests include:

- Aerodynamic breakup of axisymmetric and asymmetric droplets
- Jet formation in hollow and compound droplet impacts
- Force scaling laws and pressure dynamics during drop impact
- Drop–bubble interactions and related topological transitions
- Fish schooling dynamics using IBAMR with adaptive mesh refinement
- Bio-inspired flow mechanics and fluid–structure interaction simulations

Feel free to explore my published work, current research projects, and contact information. I am always open to academic collaboration and scientific discussions.
