I am a computational fluid dynamics (CFD) researcher specializing in **multiphase and interfacial flows**, with a focus on **drop and bubble dynamics**, **aerodynamic breakup**, and **topological transitions** in multiphase systems.

Currently, I am a **Postdoctoral Researcher** at **Zhejiang University** (since August 2025), working on the application of **CFD in Recirculating Aquaculture Systems (RAS)**. I develop and apply CFD models to optimize water flow, temperature distribution, and waste dispersion, ensuring optimal water quality and fish welfare while enhancing system efficiency and sustainability.

My previous research at **IIT Hyderabad** involved **high-fidelity direct numerical simulations (DNS)** of the aerodynamic breakup of **non-spherical droplets**, utilizing the **Volume-of-Fluid (VOF)** method implemented in the **Basilisk** framework.

During my PhD at **Xi'an Jiaotong University**, I investigated the complex dynamics of **air-in-liquid compound droplets**, revealing intricate jetting, cavity collapse, and vortex-driven deformations.

This site presents my **research publications**, **simulation visualizations**, **technical skills**, and **curriculum vitae**. My core research directions include:

- Application of CFD in aquaculture systems (RAS)
- Aerodynamic breakup of axisymmetric and asymmetric droplets
- Jet formation in hollow and compound droplet impacts
- Force scaling laws and pressure dynamics during drop impact
- Drop–bubble interactions and their topological transitions
- Grid-adaptive numerical simulations using Basilisk framework

Feel free to explore my published works, ongoing projects, and contact details. I'm always open to academic collaborations and research discussions.
