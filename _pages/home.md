I am a computational fluid dynamics (CFD) researcher with expertise in multiphase and interfacial flows, focusing on drop and bubble dynamics, aerodynamic breakup, topological transitions, and more recently, bio-inspired flow simulations.

Currently, I am a **Postdoctoral Researcher** at **Zhejiang University** (since August 2025), working on the application of **CFD in Recirculating Aquaculture Systems (RAS)**. I develop and apply CFD models to optimize water flow, temperature distribution, and waste dispersion, ensuring optimal water quality and fish welfare while enhancing system efficiency and sustainability. Alongside this, I am developing fish schooling simulations using the **IBAMR** open-source software framework, employing immersed boundary methods with adaptive mesh refinement (AMR) to study hydrodynamic interactions and collective swimming behavior.

My previous research at **IIT Hyderabad** involved **high-fidelity direct numerical simulations (DNS)** of the aerodynamic breakup of **non-spherical droplets**, utilizing the **Volume-of-Fluid (VOF)** method implemented in the **Basilisk** framework.

During my PhD at **Xi'an Jiaotong University**, I investigated the complex dynamics of **air-in-liquid compound droplets**, revealing intricate jetting, cavity collapse, and vortex-driven deformations.

This site features my research publications, simulation visualizations, technical skills, and curriculum vitae. My main research interests include:

- Application of CFD in aquaculture systems (RAS)
- Fish schooling dynamics using IBAMR with adaptive mesh refinement
- Bio-inspired flow mechanics and fluid–structure interaction simulations
- Aerodynamic breakup of axisymmetric and asymmetric droplets
- Jet formation in hollow and compound droplet impacts
- Force scaling laws and pressure dynamics during drop impact
- Drop–bubble interactions and their topological transitions
- Grid-adaptive numerical simulations using Basilisk framework

Feel free to explore my published work, current research projects, and contact information. I am always open to academic collaboration and scientific discussions.
