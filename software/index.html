Open-Source Software

Solver modules and applications for open-source CFD frameworks, available at github.com/vinodthale.

  • Basilisk (Basilisk C): drop-impact, aerobreakup, phase-change, and thermocapillary modules, with axisymmetric and 3D post-processing tools.
  • IBAMR (C++, SAMRAI, ConstraintIB): fish-swimming, scalar-transport, and particle-tracking applications, with Lagrangian mesh-generation and force diagnostics.
