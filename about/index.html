Research background

I work on computational fluid dynamics, with an emphasis on multiphase flows, interfacial dynamics and fluid–structure interaction. My doctoral work at Xi’an Jiaotong University focused on the numerical modelling of air-in-liquid compound drops, including impact forces and jet formation.

At Zhejiang University, my research applies CFD methods to agricultural engineering and aquaculture, including drop impact and fish hydrodynamics. My work uses Basilisk for adaptive multiphase simulations and IBAMR for immersed-boundary applications.
