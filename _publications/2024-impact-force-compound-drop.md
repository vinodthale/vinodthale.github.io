This study investigates the dynamics of impact forces generated by an air-in-liquid compound drop striking a liquid pool. Using direct numerical simulations (DNS) with the Basilisk CFD solver, the research explores the interplay between drop structure and impact conditions. The results reveal scaling laws for maximum impact force and highlight how the presence of a central air bubble modifies the force profile. Applications include sea spray aerosol generation and plasma spraying of thermal barrier coatings.
